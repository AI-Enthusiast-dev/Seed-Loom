How a tray grows, day by day

The seven-day cycle

01Day one

Sow

An inch of moist coco peat, levelled. Scatter the seed thickly — far denser than a vegetable bed — and press it in without burying it.

02Days two – three

Germinate

Cover the tray and keep it dark. Humidity does the work: roots find the medium and the first white shoots break the seed coat.

04Days four – five

Green up

Uncover and move to indirect light. Pale stems turn green within hours. Mist twice daily and rotate the tray so they grow straight.

07Day seven

Harvest

First true leaves appear. Cut just above the medium with scissors, rinse, and eat the same hour. Start the next tray the same evening.
